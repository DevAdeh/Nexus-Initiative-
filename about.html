About us

The Nexus Initiative works for a Nigeria where every child can learn.

Our mission

The mission of the Nexus Initiative is to advocate for quality education for all, especially underprivileged children, and to empower both children and young adults with the skills and resources they need to thrive. We are committed to fighting against gender-based violence and abuse and equipping future changemakers with the tools they need to become advocates and leaders in their communities.

Our vision

Our vision is to create a world where every individual has access to quality education, where gender equality is the norm, and where empowered changemakers drive positive societal transformation globally.
